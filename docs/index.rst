Understanding Subscription Options
----------------------------------

Your Newspapers.com login account provides access to different subscription levels, each offering varying degrees of access to the vast archive. Understanding these options helps you choose the plan that best suits your research needs.

The platform offers two main subscription types: archive-specific subscriptions and the comprehensive Newspapers.com Publisher Extra subscription [citation:3][citation:8]. Archive-specific subscriptions provide unlimited access to the pages of a particular newspaper or a small group of associated newspapers. For example, subscribing to the Journal & Courier Archive gives you access to over one million pages from that specific publication and its affiliates, dating back to 1833 [citation:3].

However, for researchers seeking broader access, the Newspapers.com Publisher Extra subscription is the most popular choice. This plan provides unlimited access to over one billion pages from more than thirty thousand newspapers, dating back to 1690 [citation:3][citation:8]. It includes papers that are still under copyright, typically those from 1930 or 1931 onward. This means you can explore not only historic publications but also more recent news coverage [citation:3][citation:8].

What makes clippings particularly special is their accessibility. Once you create a clipping using your Newspapers.com login account, it becomes publicly accessible through a unique URL.
